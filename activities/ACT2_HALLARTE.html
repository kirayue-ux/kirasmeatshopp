MY FAVORITE POEM

Forgiveness Is Not Owed

I showed them my weakness and lowered my guard,
Let them see the pieces I kept hidden and scarred.
But they proved exactly why I should’ve stayed cold,
Why some hearts don’t deserve the trust that they’re told.

I’ll cut off anyone who chooses to do me wrong,
Family or friends—I won’t pretend they still belong.
I’ll protect myself, no matter who I have to lose,
And some wounds I’ll carry forever, with no forgiveness to choose.

Written by: Hallarte, Kierah Abigail F.
